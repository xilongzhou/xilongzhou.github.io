Research Interests
My research goal is to develop a controllable and robust generative framework capable of producing virtual worlds that are perceptually indistinguishable from the real world. Toward this goal, my work spans the intersection of generative AI, computer graphics, computer vision, and deep learning. In the past, my research focused on neural rendering, as well as material acquisition and generation. Recently, my work has primarily centered on Generative AI for Video and 3D Content.

In summary, my research interests include (but are not limited to):
  • Computer Graphics, Computer Vision, Generative AI, and Deep Learning
  • Neural Rendering, Inverse Rendering, Relighting, and Appearance Modeling/Editing
  • Generative AI for Material, 3D Content, and Video
  • Novel View Synthesis, and 3D Representation
I am open to collaboration, feel free to reach out if you are interested!
